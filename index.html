01About

Steady hands,
long horizon.

I am a science student from Seoni preparing for professional pilot training. My Class 12 grounding in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics sits alongside initial aviation medicals and the first two DGCA ground subjects — a start, not a finish line.

What I bring is temperament as much as academics: calm under time pressure, respect for procedure, and the habit of arriving prepared. I want to train to the standard where safety and professionalism are automatic, not something switched on for an exam.

To develop into a safe, responsible and confident pilot, committed to high standards of professionalism and teamwork.
